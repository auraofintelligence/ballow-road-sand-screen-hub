A public multi-page concept site for a proposed community-facing hub at 10-12 Ballow Road, Dunwich / Goompi, Minjerribah.

**Public site:** <https://auraofintelligence.github.io/ballow-road-sand-screen-hub/>

The site keeps the main focus on 10-12 Ballow Road while linking nearby but separate ideas:

- Ready S.E.T. co-op trust at 9 Ballow Road.
- Dunwich ferry terminal upgrade context.
- Straddie night markets and noticeboard/onboarding workflow.
- Point Lookout and possible Amity sand-sport sites after site-by-site vetting.
- Film club, outdoor cinema and grant-readiness pathways.

## Site map

- `index.html` - public doorway and concept summary.
- `site.html` and `evidence-map.html` - site questions, photos and a browser-based 360 evidence map.
- `sports.html` and `youth-elders.html` - sand-sport and intergenerational participation ideas.
- `film.html` - screen culture, film-club and outdoor-cinema pathways.
- `markets.html` - night-market and small-trader ideas.
- `trust.html` - stewardship and decision-making questions.
- `funding.html` - staged evidence and grant-readiness work.
- `nearby.html` - clearly separated neighbouring projects and possible future sites.
- `sources.html` - source trail and evidence boundaries.

## Public boundary

This is a public concept site. Site control, sale status, approvals, cultural programming, final budgets, final designs and partner commitments belong in the next due-diligence lane.

## Local development

Open `index.html` directly in a browser, or serve the folder with a simple static server.
