Sand sports

Make sand sport the main reason people show up.

Ballow Road can become the island's everyday sand-sport base: easy to find, easy to book, easy to set up, and adaptable enough for kids, clubs, tourists, schools, older locals and 2032-ready events.

The main use

Turn one managed sand base into many reasons to visit.

Grass ovals sit quiet too often. A managed sand surface gives the island a different kind of activity: faster to mark, easier to rotate between codes, fun for mixed groups, and useful for training, social sport, markets and film nights.

The pitch is simple: build a reliable sand-sport club at Ballow Road, then use Amity and Point Lookout as satellite or showcase sites when the format fits.

Primary base

Ballow Road

Bookings, beginner sessions, equipment storage, school groups, night-market sport, cinema tie-ins and visitor orientation.

Satellite

Amity reserve

Community club link, cricket heritage, casual sport days and village-scale local trials.

Showcase

Point Lookout reserve

Finals, demo matches and special events with traffic, noise, camping and neighbours handled well.

Why a managed sand base works

Give the sport a proper home.

A beach is beautiful. A club needs fixtures, storage, shade, water, lights, toilets, safe paths, bookings, maintenance and a place for people to sit. That is what turns casual play into a repeatable community asset.
