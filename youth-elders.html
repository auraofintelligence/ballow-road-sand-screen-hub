Youth and Elders

The hub should feel useful before it feels impressive.

For young people, it can create real jobs-to-practise. For older residents and Elders, it should offer comfort, dignity, digital confidence, social connection and a place that does not feel like it was designed for someone else.

Youth pathway

Give young people useful roles, not just speeches about opportunity.

Disenfranchised youth can smell hollow programs from a mile away. The hub should offer practical, supervised, paid-or-creditable roles that lead somewhere.

Sport crew

Court setup, gear care, referee basics, coaching support and safety checks.

Market crew

Stall setup, QR notices, visitor directions, pack-down and simple customer support.

Media crew

Camera, sound, interviews, editing, permissions and public notice clips.

Tech crew

Phone help, scam awareness, forms, device setup and patient digital support.

Older residents

An island with many older residents needs comfort baked into the plan.

The median-age reality means the hub cannot be designed only around tourists, athletes and noisy youth events. It needs seating, shade, toilets, readable signs, low-glare lighting, safe walking surfaces, quieter hours and genuine respect for people who move more slowly.

Rest firstBenches, shade, water, toilets and shorter walking loops are infrastructure, not decoration.
Digital confidenceMarkets and cinema nights can include gentle help with phones, scams, forms and public notices.
Social repairSmall repeated gatherings can matter more than one big showy launch.

Wellbeing lane

Health and wellbeing should stay grounded.

The hub can support movement, social connection, food, recovery, support groups and referral-friendly programs. It should not make clinical promises or publish private health details.

Wellbeing builders

Public/private boundaries, venue checks, grant readiness and co-op health access notes.

Amity outdoor fitness

Outdoor mobility, balance, safer access, water and traffic-calming planning.

Strange but True

Plain tech, AI, scam safety, grants and local practical help.
