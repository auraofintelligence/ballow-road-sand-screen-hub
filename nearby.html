Nearby, not the same

The neighbouring ideas matter, but they should not blur into one giant claim.

10-12 Ballow Road is the main site for this proposal. 9 Ballow Road, the ferry upgrade, the maker-space pitch, Amity and Point Lookout are related context, not proof that all projects are approved or bundled.

Neighbouring map

Each place gets a cleaner job.

The distributed model is useful because it reduces pressure on any one place. It becomes dangerous if the public copy makes every site sound captured by one plan.

Learning from pressure

Point Lookout can host attention, but attention has costs.

The old Island Vibe pattern matters because it shows how camping, traffic, noise, venue pressure and governance can make or break events. The Ballow Road idea should learn from that instead of pretending a beautiful location solves logistics.

Daily baseDunwich is better for arrival, logistics, public notices and all-weather access.
IncubatorAmity can test lower-pressure sand sport and cinema formats if local support exists.
ShowcasePoint Lookout should be reserved for high-value moments with serious traffic and noise planning.
