Learning from pressure

Point Lookout can handle attention when the logistics are respected.

The old Island Vibe pattern matters because it shows how camping, traffic, noise, venue pressure and governance can make or break events. The lesson is practical: make Dunwich the daily base, use Amity for village-scale trials, and save Point Lookout for moments with a strong plan.
