Screen culture

The screen layer turns activity into memory, skills and shared story.

Outdoor cinema can start gently. Film club, youth media, documentary notes and festival nights can grow only as trust, permissions and practical skills grow.

Sand and screen

Sport gives the story something to follow. Film gives the sport a longer life.

A sand-sport day can become a youth media brief. A night market can become a trader profile. A local elder interview can become a private family archive or a public story only if approved. The point is not content extraction; it is capability.

Festival pathway

Make the festival modest before making it famous.

A careful island film festival should begin with screenings, local shorts, school/youth pathways, source-aware documentary practice and strong public/private boundaries. Bigger gala moments can wait until the community actually wants them.
