The tightrope

Build a visitor economy that locals can still live with.

The island needs more than another glossy tourism pitch. The hub should feel useful to locals first: a safe place to meet, move, eat, learn, watch films, sell small goods, ask questions and help visitors understand where they are.

That means plain language, elder-friendly seating and shade, youth-friendly work pathways, careful cultural boundaries, realistic transport notes and no pretending that every nearby idea belongs to one mega-project.

Families and EldersComfort, shade, toilets, seating, low noise and respectful timing matter as much as the big idea.
Youth and local workersSport, filming, stall setup, tech help, hospitality and event support can become real entry-level experience.
TouristsVisitors need clear, kind guidance: ferry times, last return, local conduct, spend-local options and bad-weather plans.
