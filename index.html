The offer

Make the gateway useful before it gets glossy.

The island deserves more than a tourism poster. The hub can be a working place to meet, move, eat, trade, watch films, play sport, hear music, find visitor information and give young people real jobs to practise.

The winning version is confident and adaptable: strong enough to host sport and festivals, practical enough for older locals, and clear enough that visitors know how to behave before they wander off the ferry.

Families and older localsShade, toilets, seating, readable signs, earlier hours and quieter corners make the place usable.
Youth and local workersSport, filming, stall setup, tech help, hospitality and event support become real entry-level experience.
VisitorsFerry times, last return, local conduct, spend-local options and bad-weather plans sit right where people arrive.
