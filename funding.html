Funding and governance

Big dream, grown-up delivery.

The money stack has to carry sport, markets, cinema, maintenance, staff, insurance, cultural guidance, public benefit and enough earned income to keep the place alive after the launch photos.

Staged path

Start smaller, prove use, then ask for bigger money.

Public confidence grows when the concept can run a modest version first: market trials, cinema nights, beginner sport sessions, visitor notices and youth crew practice before permanent capital works.

Stage 1: Evidence

Run temporary activations, collect feedback, test transport notes, record costs and sharpen the public brief.

Stage 2: Core build

Secure site control, approvals, basic courts, shade, storage, lighting, toilets, paths and market/cinema infrastructure.

Stage 3: Network

Add Amity and Point Lookout satellites, film/festival pathways, school programs and partner events after proof.

Funding stack

Stack the money. Keep the books clean.

The likely path combines state and federal grants, sport and recreation programs, multicultural and youth funding, 2032 legacy alignment, sponsorship, venue hire, market fees, film/cinema income and local co-op participation.
