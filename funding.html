Funding and governance

Keep the money useful, visible and local.

This is not a $41M ferry add-on and it is not an Olympic bid. It is a nearby public-use idea that can ride those bigger currents carefully: sand sport, notices, markets, screen nights, local jobs and updates people can actually check.

Right-sized asks

What is the right funding lane?

The funding case is strongest when it asks for the pieces 10-12 Ballow Road can genuinely carry, then leaves the Junner Street ferry works, park/master-plan questions and Olympic machinery in their own lanes.

Ferry upgrade companion?

What local notices, shaded pause points, visitor orientation, market activity and wayfinding could sit between 10-12 Ballow Road and the Junner Street terminal area without pretending to control it?

Real 2032 legacy?

Which sand sports, youth roles, accessible sessions and club partnerships are real enough to test with existing sport communities, including Sharks/Allsports, before anyone uses the word legacy too loudly?

Operating money?

What pays for cleaning, insurance, shade, bookings, repairs, waste, power, weather calls and staff after the first round of excitement wears off?

Plain public updates

Make every idea easy to check.

A useful noticeboard says what is being suggested, where it is, who may care, what stage it is at, what changed today and where the source is. Residents, visitors, clubs, traders and councillors should not need the backstory to follow the work.

Governance

What keeps the money conversation believable?

The public page can stay plain. The formal work still has to answer entity fit, insurance, tax, leases, child safety, cultural governance, financial reporting and who is actually responsible.

People

Who holds the responsibility?

Applicant, auspice partner, committee, co-op lane or council/community partner.

Culture

Who needs to guide this part?

Country, cultural programming, language, story and filming need the right relationships.

Money

What money is separate?

Public grants, sponsorship, earned income, donations, volunteer effort and in-kind support.

Safety

What can be run calmly?

Events, food, alcohol, weather, first aid, children, crowd flow and incident reporting.

Grant readiness

Let daily updates feed the grant pack.

The public story can come from real movement: attendance, transport issues, weather calls, partner notes, quote requests, letters of support, risk controls and what changed because locals said something useful.
